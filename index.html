The meaning behind the music

Grace is the heart of this song.

“Adom” means “grace” in Twi, a language widely spoken in Ghana. This song expresses gratitude to God for the strength, mercy and hope that carry us through life.

I chose this song because Ghanaian gospel music brings together powerful voices, joyful melodies and an encouraging message.

“Every day is another opportunity to be thankful.”
